Purpose

The purpose of this project is to provide educational information about Dr. B. R. Ambedkar and to demonstrate basic web development skills through the creation of an informative webpage.

About Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar played a pivotal role in promoting social equality, fighting discrimination, and drafting the Constitution of India. His contributions continue to inspire millions around the world.
